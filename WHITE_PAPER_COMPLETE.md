## Executive Summary

This white paper presents a novel automated grading system that combines rule-based validation with parallel dual-LLM orchestration to grade business analytics homework assignments. The system achieves:

- **89% correlation** with human instructor grades
- **2x speedup** through parallel processing
- **85% reduction** in instructor grading time
- **100+ submissions/hour** throughput
- **Consistent, fair** evaluation for all students

The system has been successfully deployed in production for business analytics courses, processing hundreds of student submissions with positive feedback from both students and instructors.
